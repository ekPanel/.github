# Code of conduct

ekPanel is built by people from many countries, in many languages, with every level of
experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for all of them.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
and applies to every ekPanel repository, issue, pull request, discussion, chat and event,
and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Our pledge

We make participation free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, social and
economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new, and with people writing in a
  language that is not their first.
- Respect different opinions and experiences; disagree about ideas, never about people.
- Give and accept helpful feedback gracefully. Say sorry when you got something wrong, and
  learn from it.
- Think about what is best for the whole community, not only for you.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insults, put-downs, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment in public or in private
- Publishing other people's private information, such as their address or email, without
  their explicit permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, write to **ekpanelbd@gmail.com**. Every report is read
and handled privately; we protect the privacy of whoever reports.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide what to do, from a private word to a temporary or permanent ban from
the project's spaces, guided by the [Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
